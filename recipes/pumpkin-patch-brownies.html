Pumpkin Patch Brownies Recipe

Pumpkin patch brownies are sure to be a hit at your autumn or halloween party as this sweet treat has a little bit of something for everyone!

Ingredients

  • 1 box brownies mix, plus ingredients called for on box
  • 1 cup white chocolate chips
  • 1 Tbsp. vegetable oil
  • Green food coloring
  • 1 can chocolate frosting
  • 1 cup crushed Oreos
  • Pumpkin candies

Steps:

  1. Preheat oven to 350° and line a 9-x-12' pan with parchemnt paper then grease with cooking spray. Prepare brownies batter according to package instructions. Pour batter into prepared pan and bake until a toothpick has moist crumbs, 28 to 30 minutes. Let cool completley.
  2. In a small bowl, combine white chocolate chips, vegetable oil and green food coloring. Microwave in 30-second intrevals until melted. Stir until smooth then transfer to a small ziplock bag and cut the top of one corner to pipe.
  3. Spread chocolate frsoting over the brownies, then sprinkle with crushed Oreos. Using the green chocolate, pipe vines over the surface of the brownies, then scatter pumpkin candies on top of the brownies.
