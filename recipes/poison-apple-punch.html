Poison Apple Punch for Halloween

Warm apple cider punch for autumn Vibes

This warm apple cider drink is perfect for a crisp Autumn evening or a haunting Halloween night.

Ingredients

  • 4 thick slices of peeled fresh ginger
  • 6 cups apple cider
  • Zest of 1 orange
  • Zest of 1 lemon
  • 1 to 3 cinnamon sticks
  • 3 whole cloves
  • 1 red apple, sliced into 8 wedges for garnish
  • Gummy worms for garnish
  • 1 750-ml bottle sparkling cranberry-apple juice
