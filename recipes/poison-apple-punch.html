Poison Apple Punch for Halloween

Warm apple cider punch for autumn Vibes

This warm apple cider drink is perfect for a crisp Autumn evening or a haunting Halloween night.

Ingredients

  • 4 thick slices of peeled fresh ginger
  • 6 cups apple cider
  • Zest of 1 orange
  • Zest of 1 lemon
  • 1 to 3 cinnamon sticks
  • 3 whole cloves
  • 1 red apple, sliced into 8 wedges for garnish
  • Gummy worms for garnish
  • 1 750-ml bottle sparkling cranberry-apple juice

Steps:

  1. Smash the ginger slices with the flat side of a chef's knife and transfer to a medium sacuepan. Add the apple cider, orange zest, lemon zest, cinnamon sticks and cloves. Bring to a simmer over medium heat. Simmer 10 minutes, then strain through a fine-mesh sieve iinto a pitcher. Let cool to room temperature, then refigerate utnil chilled, about 1 hour.
  2. Pole holes in the apple wedges with a chopstick or skewer and insert a gummy worm into each. Add the cranberry-apple juice to the punch. Serve over ice. Garnish with the apples.
